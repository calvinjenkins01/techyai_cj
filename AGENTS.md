# techyai_cj: the funnel and knowledge base

This repo holds the guide pages (docs/), the daily SMS schedule, the X
autoposter and the content rules in CLAUDE.md. It is not where videos or
carousels are made.

Studio work (the weekly run, videos, carousels, new looks, anything under
studio/, scripts/ or video-projects/) lives in `~/dev/hyperframes-editor`.
If you were asked to do studio work here, stop and say so: open that folder
and read its AGENTS.md instead. The prompts for either assistant are in
`~/dev/hyperframes-editor/studio/PROMPTS.md`.

Before writing anything here, read CLAUDE.md: no dashes of any kind in any
published text is the first rule.
