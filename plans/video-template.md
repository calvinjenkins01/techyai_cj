# The video template

Measured off @kallawaymarketing reel frames, Aug 4 2026. Canvas 1080x1920.
Build this once in CapCut, save the draft as "TEMPLATE do not edit", and
duplicate it for every video. The point is that only the middle changes.

## The four zones
- Title card, top, from about y=180. Two lines, second line on a green bar.
  Stays on screen the whole video.
- Visual zone, y=340 to y=1180. The only thing that changes. Screenshots,
  generated b roll, diagrams.
- Caption word, y=1290, sitting just above the face card. One or two words.
  Not at the bottom of the frame where most people put captions.
- Face card, bottom. A 16:9 clip at 960x540 with rounded corners and a black
  margin around it. Not full bleed.

## CapCut settings for the face clip
Scale 89 percent, which makes a 16:9 source exactly 960 wide.
Position X 0, Position Y plus 660.
Mask, Rectangle, corner radius around 24.

## The fixed kit, never changes between videos
- Background: scratchpad/template-bg.png, near black with a faint green grid,
  soft green glow behind the visual zone, vignette, grain. Bottom track.
- Face card geometry above.
- Title text layer: font, size, position, green bar. Only the words change.
- Caption layer at y=1290. Keyword gets a different color than every other word.
- Colour grade on the face clip: contrast +8, highlights -10, shadows -15,
  saturation -5, temperature -5.
- Sound: whoosh under every emphasis word, placed 2 frames early. Impact on
  each new section, five max. One riser into the keyword ask. Music bed 8%.

## Changes every video
The title words, the contents of the visual zone, the spoken words. Nothing
else. If you find yourself restyling the frame, you are rebuilding instead of
duplicating.

## Emphasis text spec
Separate track from the captions. Word spans 60 to 75 percent of frame width,
mint green, centered. Animation in Scale 0.3s, out Fade 0.2s, on screen 0.8 to
1.0 seconds total. Roughly every third or fourth word.

## Pacing
Something on screen changes every 2 to 3 seconds. On a 35 second video that is
12 to 17 changes. A cut counts, an emphasis word counts, a slide in counts, a
punch in counts.

## Before export
Sound off test: watch muted, does it still make sense. If not, add emphasis
words. Eyes closed test: listen only, does it still make sense. If not, the
script is the problem.
