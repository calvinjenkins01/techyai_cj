# Kallaway frameworks, filtered for this account

Source: two Manus summaries of Kallaway's content strategy videos, Aug 4 2026.
The originals cover 40 plus frameworks aimed mostly at people who already have
an audience, an offer, and revenue. This file keeps only what applies at 74
followers with no offer. The rest is deliberately left out.

## The finding that contradicts our current format

"If you have three to five main ideas in one short form video, it is way too
much." Framework name: Overstuffing.

Cross checked against actual numbers. Top three Instagram posts by engagement
are FLOW at 51.4 percent, SLIDES at 50.0, CODE at 47.3. All three are ONE idea.
The five item videos, SETUP and Part 3, sit near the bottom and pulled zero
keyword comments between them.

The flagship countdown series does not have to die for this. The series works
because of the numbering and the visible catalog, not because each episode
contains five items. Part 4 can be one thing explained properly and still be
Part 4.

## Confirms what we already do
- Value line must land in the first 7 to 10 seconds of a short form video.
  Our keyword at the 60 percent mark is still too late by this standard.
- Three hook alignment: visual, spoken, and text hooks must all point at the
  same subject. We already run spoken plus on screen. Add the visual.
- Sixth grade vocabulary. Matches the voice rule already in CLAUDE.md.
- Visual first thinking. The brain reads visuals faster than words, which is
  the same conclusion we reached from SLIDES beating CODE.
- Pick one topic and do not deviate. The Instagram audit found a stranger
  cannot tell whether this account is for developers or for business owners.

## New and worth adopting
- OUTLIER MINING. Pick 10 to 20 creators serving the same viewer. Find their
  10x view outliers, meaning videos with ten times that creator's own average.
  Remix the idea rather than the execution. Budget one hour a week.
  Tool mentioned: Sort Feed, re-sorts a profile by view count.
- THE FIVE LEGO BRICKS: format, idea, hook, script, visuals. Hold four constant
  and change exactly one per video. Our eight reels used five different cover
  treatments, which means every brick moved at once and nothing was learned.
- TRIAL REELS on Instagram. Runs variations that only strangers see. Built for
  an account this size.
- IDEA TARGETING MIX, 2 2 1 0. Two core topic, two adjacent, one outer, zero
  from the far edge. A weekly slate rule.
- Do not build the channel around yourself as the niche. Build it around one
  topic. Directly supports putting Claude in the name field.
