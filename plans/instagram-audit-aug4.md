## Reels versus carousels on Instagram
Reels average 445.6 views and 331.1 reached. Carousels average 139.3 views and
60.0 reached. Reels win on every measure including raw saves.

But per person reached, carousels are saved three times more often, 1.67 percent
against 0.57 percent. Two of four carousels got zero external distribution and
never left the follower graph.

Conclusion: carousels convert the few people who see them. They are not being
shown. On Instagram they are a follower retention play, not a growth play.
This is Instagram specific. TikTok photo posts behave differently.
