## Absolute writing rules

1. NEVER use a dash of any kind in anything published. No hyphen, no en dash,
   no em dash. Use commas, periods, colons, or reword. This applies to scripts,
   captions, slide text, DMs, articles, everything.
2. No ellipses, no trailing pauses, no written in hesitation, no "um" or "uh",
   no "like" as a hedge.
3. Banned phrases: "here's what nobody tells you", "this changes everything",
   and any press release phrasing.
4. Voice: write like he is telling ONE friend about something he just found.
   Contractions always. Short fragments for emphasis. At least one real
   reaction per script ("here's what got me", "no exaggeration", "I wasn't
   ready for this"). Casual connectors. Confident, not rambling. Test: read it
   aloud, if it feels like reading, rewrite it.

## Hard content rules derived from that data

- 35 seconds maximum for a video. Tutorials discovered through search may run
  longer. A 74 second video got zero comments and zero keyword conversions.
- ONE idea per video. Not five.
- Two layer hook: the SPOKEN hook carries the searchable phrase, the ON SCREEN
  text is a separate short curiosity line, 4 to 8 words.
- The cover makes a claim about the VIEWER, never about the tool. Never open a
  cover with Claude, ChatGPT, or "AI agents" as the first words.
- Frame one must have fully readable hook text. No typewriter effect, no fade
  in, no empty box.
- The keyword ask is spoken around the 60 percent mark, never only at the end.
- Ask for the FOLLOW, not just the comment. He has never once asked.
- Every caption starts with the comment keyword line.
- Instagram 3 to 4 hashtags. TikTok 4 to 5.
- Proof every burned in caption for "Claude". Auto captions render it as
  "Cloud" and "Claud" and that shipped on three of eight reels.
- Every video ends with a usable takeaway plus an engineered CTA.

## Visual system

Palette: near black background, mint green #56F091, white. Nothing else.

Carousels, 1080x1350. Four part sandwich per slide: headline in bold white
caps with one phrase flipped to green serif italic, a body line, a visual, a
closing line with another green italic phrase. Six to eight slides. Keyword on
the last slide. Grain over everything. Green glow behind the visual.

Video frame, 1080x1920, fixed template, only the middle changes:
- Title card top, persistent
- Visual zone middle, changes every 2 to 3 seconds
- Caption word at y 1290, just above the face
- Face card bottom: a 16:9 clip at 960x540, rounded corners, black margin
  around it. In CapCut that is Scale 89 percent, Position Y plus 660.

## The edit, seven passes

Do one layer across the whole video, then the next. Never build front to back.

1. Cut the audio only. Remove every breath and gap. Lock the length first.
2. Fill visuals. Every noun in the script gets a visual. Mark every 2 to 3
   seconds, 12 to 17 changes on a 35 second video.
3. Motion. Every clip gets a scale keyframe, base to plus 8 percent. Nothing
   sits still.
4. Text. Captions first. Then huge kinetic emphasis words on every third or
   fourth word, green, 0.9 seconds each, landing on the syllable not after it.
5. Sound. Whoosh under every emphasis word placed 2 frames early, 30 percent.
   Impact at each new section, 40 percent, five max. One riser before the CTA,
   25 percent. Music bed 8 percent.
6. Grade. Contrast plus 8, highlights minus 10, shadows minus 15, saturation
   minus 5, temperature minus 5. Glow on the green text.
7. Two tests. Sound off: can you follow it. Eyes closed: can you follow it.

## Image prompt pattern that works

Reference lock every generation to one hero image so the set matches:
"Match the lighting, color palette, grain and depth of field of this reference
image exactly. 4:5 vertical. Mint green and black only, no other colors. No
text, no letters, no logos, no watermark. Leave the top third empty.
Subject: [thing]."

Never let the model render the headline text. Type goes on separately so it
stays consistent.
